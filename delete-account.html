Delete Account - WALLRA

Users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data directly from within the WALLRA app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open WALLRA.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Deleted data

  • Account information
  • Favorites stored in cloud
  • User-related cloud data

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
