Delete Your WALLRA Account

This page is provided by Fethra Labs for WALLRA users who want to request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the WALLRA app:

  1. Open the WALLRA app.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Select "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access the WALLRA app, you can request deletion by contacting Fethra Labs at:

Request WALLRA Account Deletion

When submitting a deletion request by email, please use the email address associated with your WALLRA account so that we can verify the request.

Data Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following account-associated data is deleted as applicable:

  • WALLRA account information
  • Cloud-stored favorites
  • User-related cloud data

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
