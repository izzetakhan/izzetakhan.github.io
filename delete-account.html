Delete Account - NOXWALL

Users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data directly from within the NOXWALL app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open NOXWALL.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Deleted data

  • Account information
  • Favorites stored in cloud
  • User-related cloud data

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
